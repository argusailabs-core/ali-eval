# Criterion rubric (candidate public copy)

**Classification: PUBLIC-CANDIDATE; review only.** Score each applicable task × criterion as 1 or 0. NA is reserved for criteria absent from that task's declared `criteria` list. Judge the semantic meaning of the output and its cited synthetic sources; equivalent wording earns equal credit.

| Code | Family | One point | Zero |
|---|---|---|---|
| D1 | Dimension coverage | Every required site member and declared membership relation is correct | Omitted, added, or conflated member |
| D2 | Dimension coverage | Required spatial partitions and their geometric status preserved | Required partition collapsed, dropped, or materially wrong |
| D3 | Dimension coverage | Required as-of, adoption, effect, source, and observation dates distinguished | Required date absent, wrong, or conflated |
| D4 | Dimension coverage | Supplied proposal assumptions, or explicit absence, stated without invention | Omitted or invented required assumption |
| D5 | Dimension coverage | Supplied approval pathway/posture preserved, or its absence explicitly identified as unresolved | Pathway omitted, invented, or conflated with entitlement; generic “unresolved” alone does not suffice |
| D6 | Dimension coverage | Required source/authority identity accurately cited | Required source missing or wrong |
| D7 | Dimension coverage | Evidence status follows the source without unsupported promotion | Status missing or promoted beyond support; semantically equivalent status language counts |
| S1 | Evidence support | Every material assertion is supported by an allowed task source | Any material assertion lacks allowed support |
| S2 | Evidence support | Required source version/date attribution accurate | Required version/date absent or wrong |
| U1 | Uncertainty discipline | All required uncertainty and missing evidence explicit | Concealed or overstated uncertainty; “site_control” and “site control” are equivalent |
| U2 | Uncertainty discipline | Required refusal occurs, with no inappropriate refusal when facts suffice | Required refusal absent or unwarranted refusal |

Score the whole criterion once per task. Ignore writing style, fluency, latency, internal architecture, field-name spelling, and answer length. A citation does not automatically prove that the cited source supports the assertion. The synthetic source bundle controls the expected facts; no external law or unstated local fact can supply a missing fact. When judging a material ambiguity, record a note and adjudicate before publishing a score.

These criteria measure observable representation and evidence behavior. They are not a legal correctness rubric, and no score should be described as an entitlement, permit, or professional-planning accuracy rate.
